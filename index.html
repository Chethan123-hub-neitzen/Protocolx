How do I export a WhatsApp chat?

• iPhone: Open chat → Tap contact/group name at top → Scroll down → Tap Export Chat → Choose Without Media.

• Android: Open chat → Tap 3 dots (⋮) → More → Export chat → Choose Without Media.

• Save or send the generated _chat.txt to your computer and drop it above.
